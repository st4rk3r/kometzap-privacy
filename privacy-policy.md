# KOMETZAP Privacy Policy

**Developer:** Propelist Studios
**App:** KOMETZAP (Android, package `com.enginar.kometzap`)
**Effective date:** 22 October 2026
**Contact:** starkercss@gmail.com

## Summary

KOMETZAP is an offline game. It does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal or non-personal data. It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and no in-app purchases.

## What the app stores, and where

The game saves your high score, collected stars, upgrades, unlocked ships, medals and settings (music, sound, vibration, screen shake, language) **only on your own device**. This data never leaves your device through the app, and Propelist Studios cannot see it.

If Android backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may include this game save in your personal device backup (for example your Google account backup) so that your progress can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Android / Google under their own privacy terms. Propelist Studios has no access to it.

You can delete all game data at any time by uninstalling the app or using *Settings › Apps › KOMETZAP › Storage › Clear storage*.

## Internet access

The app does not request the Internet permission and does not connect to the internet.

## Permissions

- **Vibration:** used only for short haptic feedback during play (for example when your ship is hit or a boss explodes). You can turn it off in the game's settings.

No other permissions are requested. The app does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or any identifiers.

## Children

KOMETZAP does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

## Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, advertising or analytics services.
